ABOUT ME

Turning research papers into working software

I'm an MCA graduate from Government Engineering College, Thrissur, specializing in applied AI/ML. My work sits at the intersection of language models, computer vision, and backend engineering — I like taking a research idea and shipping it as something people can actually use.

My major project, LaTeX WriteSense AI, integrates the LLaMA3 LLM and NLLB-200 translation model behind a FastAPI microservices backend, packaged into a Chrome extension for real-time academic writing support. Before that, I built a YOLOv5 detection pipeline that improved accuracy by ~55% in foggy, low-visibility scenes.
